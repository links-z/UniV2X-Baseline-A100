================================================================================
WHY LEARNED FUSION WORKS
================================================================================

Decision Quality Analysis (Test Set, τ*=0.70):
  Accept Rate: 18.94% (conservative - prioritizes precision)
  BA Retention: 37.75% (recalls 37.75% of beneficial patches)
  HA Admission: 15.84% (only admits 15.84% of harmful patches)

Comparison vs Simple Confidence Baseline (at matched selectivity):
  Confidence (δ=0.30):  BA Retention 38.18%, HA Admission 22.72%
  Learned (τ=0.70):     BA Retention 37.75%, HA Admission 15.84%
  
  Difference: Similar recall, but 30% fewer false positives
  
Why? Learned predictor captures contextual patterns that simple 
     probability differences miss:
     • Neighborhood consistency (8 neighbors × 5 stats)
     • Spatial geometric validity (position encoding)
     • Temporal reliability (horizon-aware)
     • Local soft evidence (Pv, Pi, ΔP patterns)

✅ SAFE CLAIMS (Supported by current evidence):

     achieving 30% reduction in harmful patch admission at comparable 
     beneficial patch recall"
  
  • "Generalizes to held-out test scenes unseen during training"

❌ AVOID WITHOUT ADDITIONAL WORK:

  • "Statistically significant improvement" 
    → Requires: Multi-seed training + hypothesis testing
  
  • "Significant performance gain"
    → Use: "consistent" or "observed" instead
  
  • "Theoretical optimal" or "global optimal"
    → Correct: "patch-level Oracle" (constrained to 4×4 framework)

RECOMMENDED TERMINOLOGY:

  • Patch-level Oracle (not "optimal" or "theoretical optimum")
  • Observed improvement (not "significant" without statistical tests)
  • Held-out test scenes (emphasize generalization)
  • Consistent gain (across Val and Test)
  • Net benefit (over both Official and Ego)

KNOWN LIMITATIONS & FUTURE WORK
================================================================================

Current Limitations:

1. No statistical significance testing
   → Solution: Multi-seed training (5-10 runs) + bootstrap CI + paired tests

2. 37.4% recovery leaves room (62.6% gap remains)
   → Potential: Better architectures (CNN, attention), multi-scale patches

3. Patch-level Oracle is not global optimal
   → Note: Constrained to 4×4 patch accept/fallback framework

4. Conservative operating point (τ*=0.70)
   → Alternative: Could tune for different precision-recall tradeoffs

5. Macro-averaged metrics
   → Clarification: Documented in methodology, gives equal weight per scene

Before Submission (Recommended):
